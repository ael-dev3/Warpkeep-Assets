## Purpose

This archive preserves a compact visual-development set for future Warpkeep
menu-background and cinematic-video creation. It is a reference archive, not a
runtime package, a final compositing contract, or evidence that every depicted
design is currently integrated in Warpkeep.

## Derived discovery media

Two deterministic JPEG previews are tracked in Git and repeated inside the
ZIP: a 2400×1500 overview of the entire set and an 1800×1500 six-frame contact
sheet sampled from the supplied video. These are discovery aids only; future
production should begin from the exact sources.

No source image was repainted or generatively altered during packaging. No
absolute local source path is included in the public manifests or archive.

## Designation and boundaries

The visual material connects the Hegemony castle/menu atmosphere with The
Core's distant spire, violet energy, and machine-gothic crest language. The
green background is retained as supplied because it may be useful for future
keying tests; it is not designated as a canonical presentation background.

The package records supplied reference media and public distribution only.
Current live-game integration, canonical status of every treatment, and final
background-video direction are not asserted.
